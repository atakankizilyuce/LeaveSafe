Leave your laptop.
Stay safe.

LeaveSafe turns your phone into a remote alarm for your laptop. Run it, scan the QR code, tap Arm, and walk away. The moment anyone touches your machine, your phone goes off in your pocket — and the laptop starts screaming too.

Three steps, then you walk away

  1. 1

    Run it

    One file, no installer, no dependencies. The dashboard takes over the terminal: a QR code on the left, live status on the right.

  2. 2

    Scan it

    There is no app to install. The QR code opens a page served by your own laptop, with the pairing key already filled in.

  3. 3

    Arm it

    Three seconds, the sensors gather into a shield, and your laptop is being watched. Hold to disarm when you get back.

Six things it watches

  • PowerThe charger is unplugged or plugged in
  • LidThe lid is closed or opened
  • USBA USB device appears or disappears
  • ScreenThe display turns off or comes back on
  • NetworkAn interface or its IP address changes
  • InputSustained keyboard or mouse activity

One honest limit. The phone is the convenience, not the alarm. When its screen locks, the operating system freezes the page, so an alert may not reach it until you open it again. The laptop sounds regardless.

Get it

Homebrew puts leavesafe on your PATH and makes upgrading one command.

brew tap atakankizilyuce/tap && brew install leavesafe

WinGet ships with Windows. Scoop works too.

winget install LeaveSafe.LeaveSafe
scoop bucket add atakankizilyuce https://github.com/atakankizilyuce/homebrew-tap && scoop install leavesafe

One file, no installer, no dependencies. On Linux and macOS, chmod +x it before running.

  • Windows x64 leavesafe-windows-amd64.exe
  • Linux x64 · ARM64 leavesafe-linux-amd64
  • macOS Intel · Apple silicon leavesafe-darwin-arm64
